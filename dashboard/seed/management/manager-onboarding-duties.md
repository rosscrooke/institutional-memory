# Manager duties when onboarding someone

- Assign a buddy for the new hire's first two weeks.
- Get tagged on, and sign off, their read-only prod access ticket in `#sre-access-requests`
  (they become eligible after 2 weeks).
- Read-write access needs the tech lead's sign-off as well as the SRE's (6 weeks or more).
- Make sure they understand the blameless post-mortem process (written within 48h of a P0/P1).
