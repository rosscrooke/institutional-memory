# Your first two weeks

**Day 1:** laptop, YubiKey, email (firstname.lastname@bts-synthetic.example), Slack,
GitHub (`bts-synthetic` org) and read-only staging access. You don't get prod access on day 1;
see `policies/prod-access-policy.md`.

**Days 2–5:** your manager assigns a buddy for two weeks. They walk you through the codebase,
the git workflow, the on-call rotation and post-mortem culture.
