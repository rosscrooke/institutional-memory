Eastside pilot, week 4 of 6. Notes from the data and from talking to Elena.

**Numbers**
- No-show rate at Eastside: 17% baseline -> 11.5% in week 4. Trending the right way.
- Confirm rate (family taps confirm or replies C): 58%
- Opt-outs (STOP): 3.1%. Higher than our 2% planning assumption. Looking at why.

**Issues found**

- Reminders go out at 8am. Elena thinks 6pm the evening before would work better for working parents. Haven't tested.
