Subject: Compliance checklist and BAA

Hi Jordan,

Dr. Folau passed along your note. I'm the privacy officer for Brightpath. Before any patient data moves, I need the following:

1. **Signed Business Associate Agreement.** We have our own template, attached. We do not accept vendor-paper BAAs. Please have your legal team review and return redlines within two weeks.
2. **Data location.** All PHI must stay in US data centers. Please confirm in writing where the data and backups will live.
3. **Subprocessors.** I need a list of every third-party service that will touch patient contact info (the SMS provider, the email provider, any cloud hosting). Each needs its own BAA chain.
4. **Message content review.** I want to approve every template before it goes live. Spanish versions too, reviewed by someone fluent.
5. **Opt-out.** Families must be able to reply STOP, and it must actually stop all messages immediately, not on a nightly batch.
6. **Audit log.** We need to be able to show who sent what to whom, for at least 6 years.

One more point. Dr. Folau mentioned a pilot at Eastside. My preference is that we do not use real patient data until items 1-3 are complete. If you need to test before then, please use synthetic records.

Happy to get on a call if useful.
