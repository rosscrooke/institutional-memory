# Discovery Workshop — Brightpath Pediatrics

**[00:00] Dana:** We're here to understand how appointments are booked and why no-shows are a problem. Marcus, you flagged a number in the intake form?

**[00:20] Marcus:** Yes. Our no-show rate across the four clinics is 17 percent. Industry norm for pediatrics is closer to 9. Each no-show is roughly 140 dollars in lost revenue, and we do about 2,400 appointments a month.

**[00:55] Dana:** So roughly 57,000 a month on the table.

**[01:02] Marcus:** Right. Dr. Folau and I want to cut that in half within six months.

**[01:15] Dr. Folau:** Medically it matters too. Missed well-child visits mean missed vaccines. That's the part I care most about.

**[01:35] Elena:** From the front desk, reminders are the problem. We call families manually, two days ahead. We get through maybe sixty percent of the list. A lot of parents only text.

**[02:00] Jordan:** So the proposal is automated reminders by text and email, with a one-tap confirm or reschedule link.

**[02:15] Dr. Folau:** One thing. Anything we send has to be HIPAA compliant. No diagnosis details in the text messages, nothing like "your child's asthma follow-up." Just date, time, clinic.

**[02:40] Dana:** Agreed. We'll keep messages generic and put the secure details behind a login.

**[02:55] Elena:** Our scheduling system is called PediaBook. I don't know if it has an API.

**[03:05] Marcus:** I believe it does but we've never used it. The vendor's support is slow. I can ask our rep.

**[03:20] Dana:** That would be the biggest dependency. If there is no API, we'd have to scrape or do nightly exports, which is more fragile.

**[03:40] Dr. Folau:** What about Spanish? About a third of our families speak Spanish at home.

**[03:50] Jordan:** Spanish is in scope. We'll include bilingual templates.

**[04:05] Elena:** Can families reply "C" to confirm? That's what our old system did.

**[04:15] Dana:** Yes, two-way text is possible. We'd want to use a registered number for deliverability.

**[04:30] Marcus:** Timeline wise, we'd love to pilot at the Eastside clinic before rolling out to all four.

**[04:45] Jordan:** That's sensible. Pilot at Eastside, measure for 6 weeks, then expand.

**[05:00] Marcus:** Actions: I'll ask the PediaBook rep about API access. Dr. Folau will send the compliance checklist. Elena will give Dana a list of current reminder scripts.

**[05:20] Dr. Folau:** I'll also need to run this by our privacy officer, Gwen Tsai. She may want a BAA with you before we share any data.

**[05:35] Jordan:** Of course. We'll prepare a draft BAA.
