# Preferences and Training History — Marco Ferreira

*Compiled 2026-06-02 from intake call.*

## Hard preferences

- **Burpees: never.** Marco has stated that a session containing burpees is a
  session he will skip entirely. This is not negotiable and is not a
  fitness-limitation issue — it is an adherence issue. (It also happens to be
  contraindicated by the wrist, but the preference stands independently.)
- **No long steady-state cardio.** Anything over 10 continuous minutes of running
  or cycling in a strength session will not get done. 5k training is handled
  separately on non-lifting days.
- **Mornings only.** Sessions are at 06:30. Anything requiring a long warm-up is
  unrealistic — keep warm-ups under 5 minutes.

## What has worked before (2023–2024 block)

- **Supersets.** Pairing an upper and a lower movement kept sessions inside the
  time cap and Marco reported enjoying them.
- **Fixed weekly template.** He responded well to knowing Monday was always the
  same shape as last Monday. Novelty is not a motivator for him.
- **RPE-free prescription.** Give absolute numbers. "3 sets of 8 at 14 kg" works;
  "3 sets of 8 at RPE 7" does not.

## What has not worked

- Long, varied programmes with a new stimulus every week — he stopped in 2024
  during a block like this.
- Anything that needed equipment he did not own.
