# Injuries and Limitations — Marco Ferreira

*As of 2026-06-02. This section is authoritative and overrides programme design.*

## Right wrist — de Quervain's tenosynovitis

- **Diagnosed:** 2026-03-14 by Dr. Amaia Ruiz (GP referral), confirmed by physio
  Tomás Beltrán.
- **Status:** Under active physio. Twice-weekly sessions.

**Contraindicated — do not program:**

- Any **loaded wrist extension**. This rules out push-ups on flat palms, planks
  on hands, bench dips, front-rack positions, and burpees.
- Any exercise requiring a **thumb-loaded grip** (kettlebell racked position,
  suitcase carries with a thick handle).

**Permitted:**

- **Neutral-grip** work with dumbbells at light load (≤ 8 kg per hand) — the
  physio signed this off on 2026-05-20.
- Anything that loads the lower body or the posterior chain without hand support.
- Push-ups on **fists or parallettes** are acceptable in principle, but Marco does
  not own parallettes.

## Lower back — non-specific sensitivity

- No diagnosis, no imaging. Marco reports "a bad day about once a month" after
  long stretches of sitting.
- **Avoid loaded spinal flexion**: no weighted sit-ups, no Russian twists, no
  jackknives, no toes-to-bar.
- Hip-hinge patterns (Romanian deadlifts, hip thrusts, good mornings) are fine and
  in fact encouraged — they have historically made his back feel better, not worse.

## Standing instruction

If a session's exercise selection conflicts with anything in this document, the
document wins. Do not program around an injury by reducing the load — substitute
the movement.
