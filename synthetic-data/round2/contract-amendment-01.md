# Amendment No. 1 to Order Form AC-2023-117 — Acme Corp

*Signed 2026-05-28 by David Okafor (VP Procurement, Acme Corp) and Elena Park (VP Sales, Brightline). Effective 2026-06-01. Summary prepared by Deal Desk.*

## Background

Acme is closing two European warehouses as part of a restructuring and asked to reduce seats before renewal. In exchange for allowing a mid-term reduction (not normally permitted), Acme accepted tighter renewal terms.

## Changes

| Term | Before | After (effective 2026-06-01) |
|---|---|---|
| Seats | 2,400 | **2,100** |
| Price per seat per month | $30 | **$30** (unchanged) |
| ARR | $864,000 | **$756,000** |
| Renewal price uplift cap | 5% | **3%** |
| Non-renewal notice period | 90 days (deadline 2026-07-02) | **60 days (deadline 2026-08-01)** |
| Term end | 2026-09-30 | 2026-09-30 (unchanged) |

## Unchanged

All other terms of the MSA and Order Form remain in force, including the 99.9% uptime SLA and service-credit schedule.

## Deal Desk note

The shorter notice period gives Acme until **2026-08-01** to decide not to renew. Treat the renewal as open until then.
